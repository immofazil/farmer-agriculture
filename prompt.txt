Theme: Exploring and Building Agentic AI Solutions for the agriculture sector in India.
Challenge: Create a human-aligned, AI-powered advisor that can help farmers in India with a wide range of queries. The solutions should be accessible to users with low digital access and must use only publicly available datasets.
Queries to Address:
Irrigation timing
Seed variety selection
Weather impact
Market conditions
Access to finance and government policies


Create a Node.js Express API that accepts a POST request with a question from a farmer and responds using a dummy text (for now).
